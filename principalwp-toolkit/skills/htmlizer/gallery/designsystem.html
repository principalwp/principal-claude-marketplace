Does the system read on either surface

Four compositions drawn from Northwind's storefront checkout flow — the cart-to-receipt path a shopper walks, not the order-fulfilment pipeline that starts after payment — shown on a light surface and a navy one. Flip the surface and give one clear verdict for what you see; a design can ship in light and fail in navy. Each surface gets its own verdict, and an un-rated surface counts as Ship.
